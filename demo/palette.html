Design System — Draft 01

Bold, tonal,
wireframe.

Two voices. The shell is vanilla cream with a rosewood line drawing and carries everything that belongs to the evening as a whole. The panels are one saturated colour each, with the name set enormous in a tint of that same colour. Nothing is decorated; the scale does the work.

00

The Two Faces

Two families, both self-hosted as variable woff2 — nothing is fetched at runtime, so the invitation renders with no connection. The display face is pinned to one optical cut; that single setting is what made it readable.

Display — in use

Bodoni Moda, opsz 20, weight 500. Names, figures, the cover stack, the slant.
